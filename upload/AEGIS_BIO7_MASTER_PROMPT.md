## 3. RICE SECTOR PROTOCOLS — complete source content

These protocols are field-tested. Translate faithfully into Bahasa Indonesia for /padi sub-page.

### 3.1 General rules (apply to all rice applications)
- **Seed soaking**: 1 cap per 3 liters water, soak 24 hours.
- **Nursery spray**: 7 caps per 16L tank, spray every 5 days.
- **Tank-mix compatibility**: can be mixed with chemical insecticides/fungicides.
- **Strict prohibition**: do NOT mix with herbicides. Maintain minimum 7-day interval between BIO7 and any chemical herbicide (e.g., Paraquat, Glyphosate).
- **Urea rule**: from 30 HST onwards, solid Urea is strictly forbidden. (Broadcast or spray Urea after 30 HST thins plant epidermal cell walls, causing sheath blight, neck blast / Pyricularia oryzae, and lodging.)
- **Shelf life**: guaranteed viable through July 2032.

### 3.2 Protocol A — Super Tiller Generation (Super Anakan)
**Timing**: 15, 22, and 30 Days After Transplanting (HST/DAT)
**Objective**: trigger explosive production of productive tillers (target 35–50 stems/hill), prevent seedling chlorosis and root stagnation.

**Tank-mix per 16L knapsack sprayer:**

| Input | Dosage | Function |
|---|---|---|
| BIO7 | 5–7 caps (50–70 ml) | Microbial root colonization, biological N/P mobilization |
| Ultradap (Mono-Ammonium Phosphate) | 3–4 tablespoons (45–60 g) | Readily available P for deep root energy, active tiller initiation |
| CALNIT (Calcium Nitrate, 15.5% N, 26% CaO) | 2–3 tablespoons (30–45 g) | Nitrate-N + soluble Ca to thicken cell walls, prevent lodging |
| MgSO4 (Magnesium Sulfate) | 1–2 tablespoons (15–30 g) | Chlorophyll activator, photosynthesis under cloudy skies |
| Fitoflex (chelated micronutrient complex) | 1 sachet (2.5 g) | Trace elements (Zn, Cu, Fe, Mn, B, Mo), prevents hidden micro-hunger in acidic soils |

**Basal fertilizer (per hectare):**
- 5 HST: Urea 25 kg + Phonska 25 kg
- 10–15 HST: Urea 100 kg + Phonska 100 kg
- 30 HST (optional): Phonska 200 kg + Fertiphos 50 kg for 8 t/ha target; Phonska PLUS 200 kg + Fertiphos 50 kg for 10 t/ha target

**Application technique:**
- Spray mist toward base of plant hills and root zone
- Time: early morning (06:00–09:00) or late afternoon (15:30–17:30)
- Soil condition: moist/muddy (macak-macak), not parched
- Never spray during scorching midday sun (10:00–14:00) — UV kills beneficial bacteria
- If rain falls within 2 hours of application, re-apply at half dosage once weather clears

### 3.3 Protocol B — Panicle Extension & Disease Shield (Perpanjang Malai)
**Timing**: 40, 47 HST, and when flowers are uniform across the field
**Objective**: 99% of tillers produce panicles; suppress neck blast / kresek and sheath blight; deactivate walang sangit.

**Tank-mix per 16L tank:**

| Input | Dosage | Function |
|---|---|---|
| BIO7 | 7–10 caps | Bioconversion, supports grain filling |
| Meroke MKP (52% P₂O₅, 34% K₂O) | 5 tablespoons (≈25 g) | Phosphorus-potassium, panicle energy |
| CALNIT | 2 tablespoons (≈20 g) | Nitrogen + calcium, firm panicles |
| MgSO4 | 1 tablespoon | Photosynthesis support |
| Fitoflex | 1 sachet (2.5 g) | Complete micronutrients |
| Auksin Murni (pure auxin, PT Natural Agrisains Indonesia) | 1 sachet (5 g) | Root-stimulating hormone, strengthens uptake |

**Basal fertilizer (per hectare, 40–45 HST)**: KCl 100 kg + Fertiphos 100 kg. Target: 12 tonnes GKP/ha.

**Water management**: flood field to 5 cm depth from start of this phase until the tips of the panicles begin to yellow. Maintain continuously.

### 3.4 Protocol C — Grain Enlargement (Perbesar Bulir)
**Timing**: when panicles begin to bend/curve (1–2 sprays, 7-day interval)
**Water management**: continue flooding to 5 cm depth.

**Tank-mix per 16L tank:**

| Input | Dosage | Function |
|---|---|---|
| BIO7 | 7 caps | Hayati (biological) |
| KNO3 putih (Pupuk Crystal, 2 kg pack) | 5 tablespoons | Nitrogen-potassium for grain bulking |
| MgSO4 | 2 tablespoons | Magnesium + sulfur |
| TaniSIL (silika murni / pure silica) | 1 tablespoon | Strengthens stalk and grain |
| Giberelin Murni (pure gibberellin, hormone for flower & fruit stimulation) | 1 sachet (5 g) | Flower/fruit stimulation hormone |

### 3.5 Protocol D — Grain Filling & Maturation (Pergemuk & Matang Bulir)
**Timing**: when panicle tips start yellowing (1–2 sprays, 5-day interval)
**Water management**: drain field to dry until harvest — accelerates maturation, eases harvest.

**Tank-mix per 16L tank:**

| Input | Dosage | Function |
|---|---|---|
| BIO7 | 10 caps | Hayati (biological) |
| MerokeSOP (52% K₂O, 18% S, 1 kg) | 7 tablespoons | Potassium + sulfur for grain filling |

### 3.6 Emergency protocol — Acid soil / pH drop recovery
**Symptoms addressed**: padi kerdil (stunted), klowor, ambles (damping-off), daun merah/kuning (red/yellow leaves), sundep, akar coklat (brown root), tungro virus.

**Procedure:**
